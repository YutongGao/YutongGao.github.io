Leave a Death Poem! The Literature of Last Words留下辞世句吧！——遗言文学

烟霞第 85 期 · 2024 年Yanxia No. 85 · 2024

本次活动将分享各种有趣的临终遗言，从文学到梗到各种黑色幽默。 你知道哪些有意思的遗言？希望参与分享的朋友可以来一起讨论。 以下是一些例子： “自由，多少罪恶假汝之名以行！”——罗兰夫人临刑前 “别碰我的圆！”——阿基米德对侵入的罗马士兵 “人生五十年，与天地长久相较，如梦又似幻。”——出自幸若舞《敦盛》，更因织田信长而闻名 “平生不修善果，只爱杀人放火。忽地顿开金枷，这里扯断玉锁。咦！钱塘江上潮信来，今日方知我是我。”——鲁智深临终偈

This session will share all kinds of interesting last words, from the literary to the meme-worthy to all sorts of black humour. What interesting last words do you know? Friends who would like to share are welcome to join the discussion. Here are some examples: "O Liberty, what crimes are committed in thy name!" — Madame Roland before her execution "Do not disturb my circles!" — Archimedes to the Roman soldier who burst in "Man's fifty years, compared with the eternity of heaven and earth, are but a dream, an illusion." — from the kōwakamai Atsumori, made famous by Oda Nobunaga "In life I never cultivated good deeds, but loved only killing and burning. Suddenly the golden shackles spring open, and here the jade chains are torn apart. Ah! The tide comes in on the Qiantang River; today at last I know that I am I." — Lu Zhishen's parting verse
