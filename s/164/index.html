池莉方方，汉口武昌Chi Li and Fang Fang, Hankou and Wuchang

Chi Li and Fang Fang, Hankou and Wuchang池莉方方，汉口武昌

烟霞第 164 期 · 2026 年Yanxia No. 164 · 2026

本次分享将以具有鲜明武汉特色的两位作家，池莉和方方讲起，分享和讨论武汉和湖北的相关故事。 - 为什么湖北省简称是鄂？为什么不是荆/楚/襄/汉？ - 武汉是如何发展起来的？一座被两江劈成三块、又被一座桥硬凑起来的城，性格从何而来？ - 回到八十年代，中国的写实主义文学是如何发展的？ - 你对武汉的印象是什么？回到二十年前又是什么？

This session starts with two writers with a distinctly Wuhan flavour, Chi Li and Fang Fang, to share and discuss stories of Wuhan and Hubei. - Why is Hubei Province abbreviated as E? Why not Jing, Chu, Xiang or Han? - How did Wuhan develop? Where does the character of a city come from that was split into three by two rivers and then forced back together by a bridge? - Going back to the 1980s, how did Chinese realist literature develop? - What is your impression of Wuhan? And what was it twenty years ago?

The notes below are in Chinese, as the talks are.
