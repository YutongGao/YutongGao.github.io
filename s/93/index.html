Tolkien and the Oxford Reading Group That Nurtured The Lord of the Rings托尔金和孕育指环王的牛津读书社

烟霞第 93 期 · 2024 年Yanxia No. 93 · 2024

《指环王》是西方奇幻文学上最重要的里程碑之一。《指环王》系列的诞生和托尔金本人的学术道路和个人经历息息相关，其中最重要的孵化器是牛津大学一个叫做Inklings的社团（中文译为吉光片羽社、墨象社或者淡墨社）。许多英国著名作家、戏剧家、诗人都是这个社团的成员，其中最有影响力的就是托尔金和《纳尼亚传奇》的作者、基督教护教作家C.S. Lewis。 引起兴趣的点： 《指环王》的创作与托尔金的学术背景、神学思想和个人经历有何关系？ 《指环王》中的霍比特人、佛多、山姆、甘道夫的人物形象可能的来源是什么？ 托尔金对贝奥武夫的研究产生了什么影响？ 牛津大学的Inklings社团中有哪些人？平时聚会干什么？对《指环王》系列的创作有何影响？ 可以讨论的问题： 西方奇幻文学和中世纪的历史和文学有些什么有意思的联系？ 牛津Inklings社团和烟霞有什么相似和不同的地方？ 指环王、纳尼亚传奇、哈利波特、冰与火之歌之类的奇幻文学为什么这么火？

The Lord of the Rings is one of the most important milestones in Western fantasy literature. Its creation was closely tied to Tolkien's academic path and personal experience, and its most important incubator was an Oxford group called the Inklings (variously rendered in Chinese as "Jiguang Pianyu She", "Moxiang She" or "Danmo She"). Many famous British writers, dramatists and poets were members, the most influential being Tolkien and C.S. Lewis, author of The Chronicles of Narnia and a Christian apologist. Points of interest: How is the writing of The Lord of the Rings related to Tolkien's academic background, theological thought and personal experience? Where might the characters of the hobbits, Frodo, Sam and Gandalf have come from? What influence did Tolkien's scholarship on Beowulf have? Who belonged to the Inklings at Oxford? What did they do at their gatherings? How did they influence the writing of The Lord of the Rings? Questions we could discuss: What interesting connections are there between Western fantasy literature and medieval history and literature? How are the Oxford Inklings similar to and different from Yanxia? Why are fantasy works like The Lord of the Rings, The Chronicles of Narnia, Harry Potter and A Song of Ice and Fire so popular?

The notes below are in Chinese, as the talks are.
