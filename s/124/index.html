This time we will mainly talk about Gnosticism and the Albigensian Crusade. - Do you remember the theories of Irenaeus, Plotinus and Augustine that we discussed before? - How did scholasticism develop in France and Italy? What were the views of Aquinas, Anselm, Abelard, Roscelin, Roger Bacon, Scotus and Ockham? - The familiar crusade stories are mostly about marching on Jerusalem, but did you know that a group of "heretics" in southern France was also besieged by crusaders? - How were the Cathars related to the Paulicians and Bogomils we mentioned before? Why were the Franciscans and Dominicans able to live in harmony with the papacy? - Why did the popes at one point leave Rome and build the Palace of the Popes at Avignon? - Why did Sigismund convene the Council of Constance? Why was Hus burned at the stake?
