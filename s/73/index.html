A Flower in a Sinful Sea: Romance and Politics in the Late Qing孽海花——晚清变局中的风流与政治

烟霞第 73 期 · 2023 年Yanxia No. 73 · 2023

鲁迅在《中国小说史略》中将《孽海花》举为晚清四大谴责小说，评价其“结构工巧，文采斐然“。《孽海花》以真人真事为影射，所牵扯人物有名士名妓，达官大吏，也有仆从小厮，甚至清朝皇帝、欧洲王室、日本浪人、八国联军司令，是以当时上层知识分子的视角，写风流韵事，八卦掌故，更写历史更迭，政治脉络的一部奇书。 将要讨论的问题： 几段风流韵事与官场政治： 才子与王妃 名士与名妓 将军与苗女 清流与闺秀 傀儡皇帝与知己宠妃。 遗老争传《清夜录》——张爱玲的证言。 引起兴趣的话题： 写出“不拘一格降人才”的龚自珍的儿子何以成为第一个“带路党”？ 名妓赛金花与八国联军司令瓦德西一段情缘救中国？ 宫斗剧、武侠小说、戏说传奇中常见情节之鼻祖？

In his A Brief History of Chinese Fiction, Lu Xun counted A Flower in a Sinful Sea among the four great novels of exposure of the late Qing, praising it as "ingeniously constructed and brilliantly written". The novel is a roman à clef built on real people and events. Its cast includes famous scholars and celebrated courtesans, high officials and great ministers, servants and pageboys, and even the Qing emperor, European royalty, Japanese rōnin and the commander of the Eight-Nation Alliance. Written from the point of view of the upper-class intellectuals of the day, it is a remarkable book that records love affairs, gossip and anecdotes, and above all the turning of history and the currents of politics. Questions for discussion: Several love affairs and the politics of officialdom: The talented scholar and the princess The famous scholar and the famous courtesan The general and the Miao girl The Qingliu official and the young lady of good family The puppet emperor and his beloved consort and confidante. The old Qing loyalists vie to pass around the Qingye lu: Eileen Chang's testimony. Topics of interest: How did the son of Gong Zizhen, who wrote "choose talent without regard to convention", become the first man to guide a foreign invader? Did the famous courtesan Sai Jinhua save China through her romance with Waldersee, commander of the Eight-Nation Alliance? Is this the ancestor of plot devices common in palace-intrigue dramas, martial arts novels and fanciful historical romances?
