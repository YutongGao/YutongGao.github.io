The Hidden Threads of Chinatown: Migration, Community and Revolution唐人街的隐秘脉络：移民、社群与革命的交织

烟霞第 113 期 · 2024 年Yanxia No. 113 · 2024

旧金山唐人街不仅是这座城市最具标志性的旅游景点之一，更是一片充满故事的街区。表面上，这里散发着浓厚的东方风情，吸引着无数游客，背后却隐藏着跨越大洋的复杂历史。从早期台山人的移民浪潮，到排华法案下的艰难求存，再到二战后华人地位的转变；从宗族与堂口的运行，到黑帮的兴衰，再到孙中山的革命策划，唐人街不仅记录了华人在北美的奋斗历程，也深刻影响了近代中国的历史。本次分享将结合实地Citywalk的角度，从唐人街现存的历史建筑与街区入手，带你发现隐藏在街巷背后的故事。 - 移民潮：为什么台山人成为早期移民美国的主力？清末的社会动荡与海外的吸引力如何改变了他们的命运？ - 社群组织：六大公司与堂口如何在唐人街的日常运转中发挥作用？黑帮在资源争夺与生存竞争中扮演了怎样的角色？ - 文化与危机：1906年旧金山大地震后，唐人街如何从废墟中重生，并被重新塑造成“东方文化主题公园”？这是主动选择还是被动妥协？ - 法律与抗争：在排华法案的阴影下，华人如何通过法律与非暴力抗争保护自身权利？王清福的努力为何被称为“华人的马丁路德金”？ - 革命与联动：孙中山在唐人街如何筹划革命？这里与清末革命党之间的联系隐藏了哪些鲜为人知的细节？

San Francisco's Chinatown is not only one of the city's most iconic tourist attractions but also a neighbourhood full of stories. On the surface it exudes a strong Oriental flavour that draws countless visitors, but behind it lies a complex history that spans the ocean. From the early waves of immigrants from Taishan, to the struggle to survive under the Chinese Exclusion Act, to the change in Chinese Americans' status after the Second World War; from the workings of clan associations and tongs, to the rise and fall of the gangs, to Sun Yat-sen's revolutionary plotting, Chinatown not only records the struggles of the Chinese in North America but also profoundly shaped the history of modern China. Taking the perspective of a citywalk, this session starts from Chinatown's surviving historic buildings and streets and takes you to discover the stories hidden in its alleys. - Migration: Why did people from Taishan make up the bulk of early Chinese immigrants to the United States? How did the social upheaval of the late Qing and the lure of overseas opportunity change their fate? - Community organisations: How did the Six Companies and the tongs shape the daily running of Chinatown? What role did gangs play in the competition for resources and survival? - Culture and crisis: After the 1906 San Francisco earthquake, how did Chinatown rise from the ruins and get remade into an "Oriental culture theme park"? Was this an active choice or a reluctant compromise? - Law and resistance: Under the shadow of the Chinese Exclusion Act, how did Chinese Americans protect their rights through the law and nonviolent resistance? Why has Wong Chin Foo been called "the Chinese Martin Luther King"? - Revolution and connections: How did Sun Yat-sen plan his revolution in Chinatown? What little-known details lie hidden in Chinatown's ties with the late-Qing revolutionaries?

The notes below are in Chinese, as the talks are.
