From Late Persia to Talas: The War to End War?从后波斯到怛罗斯——The war to end war?

烟霞第 123 期 · 2025 年Yanxia No. 123 · 2025

作为营销号常客的怛罗斯之战，从阿拉伯和中亚城邦的视角看过去是什么样的？阿拉伯帝国对中亚地区的渗透和征服是一个什么样的过程？中国在八世纪的中亚地区是一个什么角色？ 作为中国古代和盛极一时的阿拉伯帝国之间的（唯一一次）交锋，公元751年发生的怛罗斯之战常常被各种营销号提及。不过，怛罗斯之战其实发生于一个比较特殊的环境中，到底能多大程度上代表唐代中原朝廷对中亚地区的态度和政策其实值得怀疑，相反此战之后阿拉伯帝国和唐朝之间交往的迅速升温或许更值得注目。 这次分享希望从多个角度描述阿拉伯帝国对中亚地区渗透和征服的过程，梳理中亚地区历史的一些脉络，聊一聊在各大强国之间生存的中亚城邦国家的外交环境和选择。

The Battle of Talas is a regular on clickbait accounts, but how does it look from the perspective of the Arabs and the city-states of Central Asia? What was the process by which the Arab empire penetrated and conquered Central Asia? What role did China play in eighth-century Central Asia? As the (only) clash between ancient China and the Arab empire at its height, the Battle of Talas in AD 751 is often brought up by all kinds of clickbait accounts. However, the battle actually took place in rather special circumstances, and it is questionable how far it represents the attitude and policy of the Tang court towards Central Asia. Rather, the rapid warming of relations between the Arab empire and the Tang after the battle may deserve more attention. This session aims to describe from several angles how the Arab empire penetrated and conquered Central Asia, trace some threads of Central Asian history, and talk about the diplomatic environment and choices of the Central Asian city-states surviving between the great powers.

The notes below are in Chinese, as the talks are.
