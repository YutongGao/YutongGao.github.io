The Great Footsteps: A Mysterious Traveller from Morocco伟大的足迹——来自摩洛哥的神秘旅者

烟霞第 100 期 · 2024 年Yanxia No. 100 · 2024

引起兴趣的问题： - 你青年时代读过《经行记》《天方夜谭》《马可波罗游记》吗？ - 辛巴达的故事是如何迭代的？ - Baṭūṭah和Batu有什么词源联系？ - 郑和下西洋的航海路线是如何得知的？ 分享的问题： - 伊本·白图泰是如何走过四十四个国家的？ - 为什么在十四世纪远距离旅行可以实现？ - 白图泰如何到访中国？留下什么印象？ - 海陆丝绸之路如何兴衰迭起？

Questions to spark interest: - Did you read the Jingxing Ji, The Arabian Nights or The Travels of Marco Polo when you were young? - How did the stories of Sinbad evolve over time? - Is there an etymological link between Baṭūṭah and Batu? - How do we know the routes of Zheng He's voyages to the Western Oceans? Questions we will cover: - How did Ibn Battuta travel through forty-four countries? - Why was long-distance travel possible in the fourteenth century? - How did Ibn Battuta come to visit China? What impressions did he leave? - How did the overland and maritime Silk Roads rise and fall in turn?
