    关于烟霞About Yanxia

    烟霞是一个线上主题分享活动，2022 年开始，通常在美西时间周五傍晚进行。每期的通知会同时发在微信公众号和 Google 群组。

    Yanxia is an online talk series that began in 2022, usually held on Friday evenings, US Pacific time. Each session is announced on the WeChat account and in the Google Group. Talks are in Chinese.
