How to Be an Aristocrat of the Southern Dynasties南朝贵族的自我修养

烟霞第 101 期 · 2024 年Yanxia No. 101 · 2024

一些引起兴趣的问题： 王谢是名声在外的世家大族，在历史上究竟出过一些什么人物？ 对于南齐这个政权，你有什么印象？ “竟陵八友”是谁？他们是做什么凑到一起的？ 南朝的寒门和高门是怎么划分的？ 你对六朝文学有什么印象？六朝时期的文学家们有什么代表作品，取得了哪些发展？ 作为琅琊王氏的一员，却经历了并不常见的家庭变故，王融的人生从一开始似乎就偏离了一个标准南朝贵族的剧本。然而在他不长的一生中，他用自己的努力重新“塑造”了一个优秀的南朝贵族，在这个期间又作为一时文坛领袖，为后世留下了“永明文学”这个宝贵的财富，影响了之后中国文学的发展走向。 尽管他没能在政治上推动与他意气相投的贵族王子萧子良登上王位，他的朋友萧衍最终还是开启了贵族化的南梁。然而，这个南朝贵族掌舵的南梁政权在侯景之乱中光速陨落，南朝的贵族也受到了北方人的嘲笑。 王融的追求，是不是从一开始就有什么问题？贵族化的南朝衰亡是不是一种必然？ 在本期活动之后我们还可以讨论这样一些问题： 为什么作为当时的文坛领袖，王融的名气后来却不如同为竟陵八友的谢朓，沈约等人？ 一个人所处的时代会怎样影响TA对文学作品的评价？ 我们该怎么看音韵声律和引用典故在文学欣赏上的意义？

Some questions to spark interest: The Wang and Xie were famously illustrious great clans; what notable figures did they actually produce in history? What impression do you have of the Southern Qi regime? Who were the "Eight Friends of Jingling"? What brought them together? How were humble families and great families distinguished in the Southern Dynasties? What impression do you have of Six Dynasties literature? What are the representative works of Six Dynasties writers, and what advances did they make? As a member of the Wang clan of Langya who nonetheless suffered an unusual family misfortune, Wang Rong's life seemed from the very start to stray from the script of a standard Southern Dynasties aristocrat. Yet in his short life he used his own efforts to "reshape" himself into an exemplary Southern aristocrat, and as a leading literary figure of his day he left posterity the precious legacy of "Yongming literature", which influenced the later course of Chinese literature. Although he failed to help Prince Xiao Ziliang, a like-minded aristocrat, onto the throne, his friend Xiao Yan eventually founded the aristocratic Southern Liang. But this Liang regime, steered by the southern aristocracy, collapsed at lightning speed in the Hou Jing Rebellion, and the southern aristocrats became a laughing stock to the northerners. Was there something wrong with what Wang Rong pursued from the very beginning? Was the fall of the aristocratic Southern Dynasties inevitable? After this session we can also discuss questions such as: Why, though he was the literary leader of his time, did Wang Rong later become less famous than his fellow Eight Friends of Jingling such as Xie Tiao and Shen Yue? How does the era a person lives in shape how they judge works of literature? How should we regard the significance of tonal prosody and literary allusion in appreciating literature?
