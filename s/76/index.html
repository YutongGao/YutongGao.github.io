From Yiguandao to Guo Jicheng: Syncretic Religion in Shandong从一贯道到郭继承——宗教习合在山东

烟霞第 76 期 · 2023 年Yanxia No. 76 · 2023

在电影《我的1919》中，陈道明饰演的顾维钧面对巴黎和会上的各国代表时说："中国不能失去山东，就像西方不能失去耶路撒冷。"本次分享将以山东为主题，讨论各宗教在山东的发展历程。 注明：现实中顾维钧说的其实是“山东省是中华文明的摇篮，孔子和孟子的诞生地，对中国人而言，这是一块圣地。” 引起兴趣的问题： 宇宙万法的那个源头是什么？（？） 传统宗教和邪教的区别是什么？ 为什么说：“中国不能失去山东，就像西方不能失去耶路撒冷”？ 你还知道哪些综摄特色的宗教？ 本次分享要讨论的问题： 各个宗教在山东发展的历程。 一贯道的出现、发展和现状。 最近互联网上一些和郭继承相关的梗。 怎么看待第三条道路？

In the film My 1919, Chen Daoming, playing Wellington Koo, tells the delegates of the powers at the Paris Peace Conference: "China cannot lose Shandong, just as the West cannot lose Jerusalem." This session takes Shandong as its theme and looks at how the various religions developed there. Note: what Wellington Koo actually said was, "The province of Shandong is the cradle of Chinese civilisation, the birthplace of Confucius and Mencius; for the Chinese it is a Holy Land." Questions to get us thinking: What is the source of all the dharmas of the universe? (?) What is the difference between a traditional religion and a cult? Why say that "China cannot lose Shandong, just as the West cannot lose Jerusalem"? What other syncretic religions do you know of? Questions for this session: How the various religions developed in Shandong. The emergence, development and present state of Yiguandao. Some recent internet memes about Guo Jicheng. What should we make of the "third way"?
