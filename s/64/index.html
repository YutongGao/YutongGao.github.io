A Poet's History of 1644: Wu Meicun甲申诗史--吴梅村书写的一六四四

吴伟业，字骏公，号梅村，是明清之际著名诗人，他于长篇歌行上的造诣，与元（稹）白（居易）的长庆体齐名；名列“江左三大家”，却也被列入乾隆年间修订的贰臣传。他一生曲折经历造就了其冠绝一时的诗艺，更因历经两朝的“亡国之人，血心流注”倾注出诗史之笔。 本次分享将由吴梅村围绕甲申之变的长篇歌行，试一窥王朝易代之际从皇族到平民各阶层的哀乐境遇，及诗歌中“推见至隐”的历史细节。 引起兴趣的问题： 梅村笔下崇祯与田贵妃，吴三桂与陈圆圆，冒辟疆与董小宛，他自己与卞玉京的风流韵事 清初三大疑案之世祖出家，在梅村诗中或有线索？ 最近冒出红楼梦作者为吴梅村的言论，尊嘟假嘟？ 讨论的问题： 何为诗史互证？ 吴梅村笔下的明末情景 亡国之叹中的人性维度

Wu Weiye, courtesy name Jungong, sobriquet Meicun, was a celebrated poet of the Ming–Qing transition. His mastery of the long song-form ballad is ranked alongside the Changqing style of Yuan Zhen and Bai Juyi. He was counted among the "Three Masters of Jiangzuo", yet was also listed in the Biographies of Twice-Serving Officials compiled under the Qianlong emperor. The twists and turns of his life shaped a poetic art unrivalled in his day, and as "a man whose country had perished, pouring out his heart's blood" across two dynasties, he wrote with the pen of a poet-historian. This session will use Wu Meicun's long ballads on the upheaval of 1644 to glimpse the joys and sorrows of every level of society, from the imperial family to commoners, at the change of dynasties, and the historical details that his poems "infer from the visible to the hidden". Questions to get us thinking: The love affairs in Meicun's poems: the Chongzhen emperor and Consort Tian, Wu Sangui and Chen Yuanyuan, Mao Pijiang and Dong Xiaowan, and Meicun himself and Bian Yujing Might Meicun's poems hold clues to one of the three great mysteries of the early Qing, the Shunzhi emperor becoming a monk? Recently the claim has surfaced that Wu Meicun was the author of Dream of the Red Chamber. For real, or not for real? Questions for discussion: What is the mutual verification of poetry and history? Wu Meicun's depiction of the late Ming The human dimension in laments for a fallen dynasty
