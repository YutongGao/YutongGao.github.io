Anime Geography 101: Kamakura and What Is Real动漫地理学101：镰仓——何为真物

烟霞第 68 期 · 2023 年Yanxia No. 68 · 2023

本次分享将讲述与日本镰仓相关的历史故事和影视作品，探究千百年来这座城市的变迁。 引起兴趣的问题： 你是否听说过镰仓这个城市？ 你是否听说过镰仓幕府，或者镰仓的历史？ 镰仓有哪些好玩的地方？ 你是否听说过《我的青春恋爱物语果然有问题。》？ 你是否听说过……算了如果你感兴趣就搜搜吧！ 将要讨论的问题： 镰仓幕府的兴亡。 佛教曹洞宗、临济宗、净土宗、日莲宗分别有什么区别？ 《春物》到底讲了一个什么样的故事？ 为什么有很多文学家，诸如芥川、川端等人，选择了在镰仓居住？ 有哪些跟镰仓相关的影视作品？ 参考资料： 《平家物语》（军记物语、同名动画） 《平清盛》（大河剧） 《太平记》（军记物语、同名大河剧） 《灌篮高手》（漫画、同名动漫） 《我的青春恋爱物语果然有问题。》（轻小说、同名动漫） 《海街日记》（电影） 《晚春》（电影）

This session will tell historical stories and look at films and series connected with Kamakura in Japan, exploring how the city has changed over the centuries. Questions to get us thinking: Have you heard of the city of Kamakura? Have you heard of the Kamakura shogunate, or of Kamakura's history? What fun places are there in Kamakura? Have you heard of My Youth Romantic Comedy Is Wrong, As I Expected? Have you heard of... never mind, if you're interested, look it up! Questions for discussion: The rise and fall of the Kamakura shogunate. What are the differences between the Sōtō, Rinzai, Jōdo and Nichiren schools of Buddhism? What story does Oregairu actually tell? Why did so many writers, such as Akutagawa and Kawabata, choose to live in Kamakura? What films and series are connected with Kamakura? References: The Tale of the Heike (war tale; anime of the same name) Taira no Kiyomori (taiga drama) Taiheiki (war tale; taiga drama of the same name) Slam Dunk (manga; anime of the same name) My Youth Romantic Comedy Is Wrong, As I Expected (light novel; anime of the same name) Our Little Sister (film) Late Spring (film)
