Fooling Yourself: What Is Fraud?自欺欺人：什么是诈骗

烟霞第 28 期 · 2022 年Yanxia No. 28 · 2022

本次分享将以近年来一些热议事件切入，探讨诈骗现象的现实原因和如何进行防骗。 诈骗的经典案例：翟山鹰（希望参与者提前了解一下，比如马前卒的视频） 睡前消息谈翟山鹰： https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=EGaGXwBsMjc 翟山鹰老师谈芯片： https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=EGpuh7SeXD4 问题： 谁是翟⼭鹰，他做了什么？他为什么能骗那么多钱？ 到底怎么样才算是⼀个骗⼦？翟⼭鹰？贾跃亭？⻢斯克？张⾸晟？他们谁算？河南村镇银⾏算吗？ 诈骗应该被杜绝吗？在现实中在什么程度上可以被杜绝？ 诈骗的本质是什么？如何防止被骗？

Starting from some much-discussed events of recent years, this session explores the real-world causes of fraud and how to guard against being scammed. A classic fraud case: Zhai Shanying (participants are encouraged to read up on him beforehand, for example through Ma Qianzu's videos) Bedtime News on Zhai Shanying: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=EGaGXwBsMjc Mr Zhai Shanying on chips: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=EGpuh7SeXD4 Questions: Who is Zhai Shanying and what did he do? How was he able to swindle so much money? What exactly makes someone a fraudster? Zhai Shanying? Jia Yueting? Musk? Zhang Shousheng? Which of them count? Do the Henan rural banks count? Should fraud be stamped out? To what extent can it be stamped out in practice? What is the essence of fraud? How can we avoid being deceived?
