The Death of Giordano Bruno布鲁诺之死——不懂玄学和哲学的神父不是好科学家

烟霞第 102 期 · 2024 年Yanxia No. 102 · 2024

本次分享关于影响力巨大的思想家、哲学家布鲁诺的生平、作品、思想，以及围绕他被判火刑而死的原因产生的巨大争论。 引起兴趣的问题： 布鲁诺到底犯了什么罪会被判烧死？ 中世纪的宗教裁判所到底是一个什么样的组织？ 日心说最早是哥白尼提出的吗？ 我们所知的“科学”概念是怎么起源的？和神学、经院哲学和神秘学有什么关系？ 拟讨论的问题： “什么是真理？”（福音书中审判官彼拉多问耶稣基督的问题） 理性和神启、科学和宗教是什么关系？ “现代社会”的主流世界观是如何一步一步形成的？ 中世纪给现代社会造成了什么样的影响？有哪些遗产被保留，而哪些遗产被否定？

This session is about the life, works and thought of the hugely influential thinker and philosopher Giordano Bruno, and the great controversy over why he was burned at the stake. Questions to spark interest: What crime did Bruno commit that got him sentenced to be burned? What kind of organisation was the medieval Inquisition? Was Copernicus really the first to propose heliocentrism? How did our concept of "science" originate? How is it related to theology, scholasticism and occultism? Questions for discussion: "What is truth?" (the question the judge Pilate asked Jesus Christ in the Gospels) What is the relationship between reason and revelation, and between science and religion? How did the dominant worldview of "modern society" take shape step by step? What impact did the Middle Ages have on modern society? Which parts of its legacy have been kept, and which rejected?

The notes below are in Chinese, as the talks are.
