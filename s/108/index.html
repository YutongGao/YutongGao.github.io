Meeting American Voters: Canvassing in Reno, Nevada直面美国选民——内华达里诺的竞选游说分享

烟霞第 108 期 · 2024 年Yanxia No. 108 · 2024

当我们观察美国大选的时候，往往会陷入新闻媒体的信息海洋。然而总统和议员不是由记者和编辑们指定的，而是由选民们一票一票投出来的。和亲友的交谈、自己所在行业的变化、社区的动员会以更根本的形式影响选民的决定，进而决定整个国家的命运。 本次活动的分享者参与了在内华达州里诺的选举游说，和各个政治光谱的选民当面交谈。与选举志愿者们接触的三天时间里，各种经历打破了他对两党支持者的刻板印象，也让他对竞选游说活动有了一些新的看法。这次沙龙，他会分享自己在内华达里诺的游说经历，叙述选民们对两党候选人的担忧和疑惑。 - 非美国公民可以合法参与美国政治吗？ - 距离竞选只有三周，真的有人仍然摇摆不定吗？ - 选举志愿者们都有哪些背景？他们为什么会参与到游说中去？ - 游说具体是如何实施的？它们的效果如何？

When we follow a US presidential election, we are often drowned in a sea of news media. Yet presidents and members of Congress are not appointed by reporters and editors; they are elected by voters, one vote at a time. Conversations with family and friends, changes in one's own industry and community mobilisation influence voters' decisions in more fundamental ways, and in turn decide the fate of the whole nation. The speaker for this session took part in election canvassing in Reno, Nevada, talking face to face with voters from across the political spectrum. Over three days working with campaign volunteers, a range of experiences broke down his stereotypes of both parties' supporters and gave him some new perspectives on canvassing. In this salon he will share his canvassing experience in Reno and describe voters' worries and doubts about both parties' candidates. - Can non-US citizens legally take part in American politics? - With only three weeks to go before the election, are there really people still undecided? - What backgrounds do campaign volunteers come from? Why do they get involved in canvassing? - How exactly is canvassing carried out? How effective is it?
