马勒与卡夫卡Mahler and Kafka

Mahler and Kafka马勒与卡夫卡

烟霞第 3 期 · 2022 年Yanxia No. 3 · 2022

本次分享将围绕作曲家马勒与作家卡夫卡的背景、作品，对于他们的思想、身份认同进行有趣的讨论。问题如下： 1.为何把马勒和卡夫卡放在一起讨论？难道是因为“作家”和“作曲家”仅有一字之差吗？ 2.作为音乐家的马勒和作家的卡夫卡有何共性？ 3.如何看待马勒交响作品中的断裂与意识形态攻伐？ 4.如何理解卡夫卡的写作理念和作品中的隐喻？ 5.在马勒和卡夫卡的作品中如何加入中国文化元素？对待中国文化，他们采取了什么样的态度？ 大家对于马勒/卡夫卡的印象是什么？或者更大的说，大家对于德国/捷克的印象是什么？

This session will explore the backgrounds and works of the composer Mahler and the writer Kafka, with a lively discussion of their thought and sense of identity. The questions are as follows: 1. Why discuss Mahler and Kafka together? Is it just because "writer" and "composer" differ by only one character in Chinese? 2. What do Mahler the musician and Kafka the writer have in common? 3. What do you make of the ruptures and ideological clashes in Mahler's symphonic works? 4. How should we understand Kafka's ideas about writing and the metaphors in his work? 5. How did Mahler and Kafka bring elements of Chinese culture into their work? What attitude did they take towards Chinese culture? What are your impressions of Mahler and Kafka? Or, more broadly, what are your impressions of Germany and the Czech lands?
