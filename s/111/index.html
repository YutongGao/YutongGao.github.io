《君主论》和《小王子》The Prince and The Little Prince

The Prince and The Little Prince《君主论》和《小王子》

烟霞第 111 期 · 2024 年Yanxia No. 111 · 2024

你听说过《小王子》这部小说吗？据说它是被翻译推广最多的一部作品。藉由名字的相关性和微乎其微的相似程度，我们来讨论一下马基雅维利的《君主论》和圣埃克絮佩里的《小王子》。 引起好奇的问题： - 《小王子》讲了一个什么故事？跟作者的经历有何关系？ - 马基雅维利是谁？为什么马基雅维利主义经常为人诟病？ - 文艺复兴是如何产生的？跟美第奇家族有什么关系？ - 你去过佛罗伦萨吗？佛罗伦萨共和国如何诞生了但丁这样的诗人？ - 你听说过电视剧《波吉亚家族》《美第奇家族》吗？ 将要分享的问题： - 《小王子》中间有哪些细节反映了作者的生平经历？ - 马基雅维利在什么情况下写下《君主论》？ - 佛罗伦萨共和国是如何落入美第奇家族之手的？ - 切萨雷·波吉亚、达芬奇在那个时代扮演什么角色？

Have you heard of the novella The Little Prince? It is said to be the most translated work in the world. Taking advantage of the connection in their names, and their very slight resemblance, we will discuss Machiavelli's The Prince and Saint-Exupéry's The Little Prince. Questions to spark curiosity: - What story does The Little Prince tell? How does it relate to the author's own life? - Who was Machiavelli? Why is Machiavellianism so often condemned? - How did the Renaissance come about? What did it have to do with the Medici family? - Have you been to Florence? How did the Florentine Republic produce a poet like Dante? - Have you heard of the TV series The Borgias and Medici? Questions we will cover: - Which details in The Little Prince reflect the author's life? - Under what circumstances did Machiavelli write The Prince? - How did the Florentine Republic fall into the hands of the Medici? - What roles did Cesare Borgia and Leonardo da Vinci play in that era?

The notes below are in Chinese, as the talks are.
