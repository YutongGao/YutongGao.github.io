宋神宗之死The Death of Emperor Shenzong of Song

The Death of Emperor Shenzong of Song宋神宗之死

烟霞第 27 期 · 2022 年Yanxia No. 27 · 2022

元丰八年三月甲午，37岁的宋神宗去世。他因为任用王安石实行熙丰变法而闻名，又在死后被推翻之前推行的所有政策。 宋神宗去世后，北宋后期以及南宋政局多次发生反复，导致《神宗实录》多次重修，因此对神宗去世、哲宗即位这一历史事件的描述有很多不同来源，不同立场的不同版本。 我们将通过简要的背景人物介绍，以及共同阅读相关叙述，来了解不同立场的人对这一事件的描述，推断叙述的可信度，并试图还原事情的真相。 相关争议要点： * 宰相王珪是否支持哲宗即位 * 次相蔡确是否有拥立哲宗的定策之功 * 宣仁皇后是否有意让次子雍王即位 * 次相蔡确是否有意支持雍王即位

On the jiawu day of the third month of the eighth year of Yuanfeng (1085), Emperor Shenzong of Song died at the age of 37. He is known for employing Wang Anshi to carry out the Xifeng reforms, and after his death all the policies he had put in place were overturned. After Shenzong's death, the political situation of the late Northern Song and the Southern Song swung back and forth many times, and the Veritable Records of Shenzong were revised again and again. As a result, there are many different versions, from different sources and different standpoints, of the events surrounding Shenzong's death and Zhezong's accession. Through brief introductions to the background and the people involved, and by reading the relevant accounts together, we will look at how people of different standpoints described these events, judge how credible each account is, and try to reconstruct what really happened. Key points of controversy: * Did the chief councillor Wang Gui support Zhezong's accession? * Did the second councillor Cai Que deserve credit for securing Zhezong's enthronement? * Did Empress Xuanren intend for her second son, the Prince of Yong, to take the throne? * Did the second councillor Cai Que intend to support the Prince of Yong's accession?
