Let the Moon Lead the Way遮莫先驱到望舒

烟霞第 66 期 · 2023 年Yanxia No. 66 · 2023

中秋节是中国最重要的传统节日之一。每年中秋，嫦娥奔月、吴刚伐桂、玉兔捣药的故事，无数诗词歌赋和各式月饼就会营造出浓浓的节日氛围。然而，如果我们以更深更广的世界视角来看待中秋神话故事，是否能发现更多以前从未注意到奇妙情节和深层意蕴？ 本次分享将由比较中国嫦娥奔月故事和西方阿波罗与达芙妮的故事说起，探讨古代神话共通的天文学含义和跨越东西方的生命旅程经验，看看东西方对于月亮、宇宙、长生、永恒各自留下了怎样的深刻见解，以及中国人眼中的月亮到底有何特别。 拟讨论的问题： 夜光何德，死则又育？ 厥利维何，而顾菟(tù)在腹？ --屈原 楚辞 天问 月亮在中国古典传统中到底有多少雅称？有哪些雅称背后暗含深意？ 嫦娥奔月究竟是为了啥？ 吴刚犯了什么罪，为啥受到这种形式的惩罚？ 白兔捣药捣的啥药？ 月亮上的桂树有何特别？ 中国人对月亮有何特别的见解？这些见解从哪里来的？

The Mid-Autumn Festival is one of China's most important traditional festivals. Every year at Mid-Autumn, the stories of Chang'e flying to the moon, Wu Gang felling the osmanthus tree and the Jade Rabbit pounding medicine, along with countless poems and all kinds of mooncakes, create a rich festive atmosphere. But if we look at the Mid-Autumn myths from a deeper and broader, global perspective, can we find wonderful details and deeper meanings we had never noticed before? This session begins by comparing the Chinese story of Chang'e flying to the moon with the Western story of Apollo and Daphne. It explores the astronomical meanings shared by ancient myths and the experience of life's journey that spans East and West, looks at the profound insights East and West each left us about the moon, the cosmos, longevity and eternity, and asks what is really so special about the moon in Chinese eyes. Questions we plan to discuss: What virtue has the light of night, that it dies and then is born again? What does it gain, that it keeps the hare (gutu) in its belly? -- Qu Yuan, Chu Ci, "Heavenly Questions" How many elegant names does the moon have in the Chinese classical tradition? Which of them carry hidden meanings? Why exactly did Chang'e fly to the moon? What crime did Wu Gang commit, and why was he punished in this particular way? What medicine is the white rabbit pounding? What is special about the osmanthus tree on the moon? What particular views of the moon do the Chinese hold? Where do these views come from?

The notes below are in Chinese, as the talks are.
