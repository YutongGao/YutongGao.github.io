Soulstealers: The Chinese Sorcery Scare of 1768叫魂：1768年中国妖术大恐慌

烟霞第 18 期 · 2022 年Yanxia No. 18 · 2022

剪掉头发，取人魂魄。一股妖风在大清盛世蔓延，一个疑案让百姓陷入了大恐慌。皇帝亲自指挥，官员纷纷出动。这个疑案到底是怎么回事？它的主谋究竟是谁？“剪辫案”发生的时期和今天的中国有什么相似之处？又有什么不同？ 本次烟霞沙龙，主讲人屈直会给大家分享读书心得《叫魂：1768年中国妖术大恐慌》，带领大家探索一个危机重重的康乾盛世。分享之前，有几个问题值得大家思考： - 你相信灵魂的存在吗？ - 你接触过哪些描绘妖术的艺术作品？ - 小说？ - 影视剧？ - 游戏？ - 你听说过哪些关于魂魄的说法？ - 你对清朝和乾隆皇帝有哪些印象？

Cut off a queue and steal a soul. An evil wind swept through the prosperous Qing, and a mysterious case threw the common people into a great panic. The emperor took personal command, and officials were sent out in force. What was really behind this case? Who was its mastermind? What does the period of the "queue-clipping cases" have in common with China today? And how is it different? In this Yanxia salon, our speaker Qu Zhi will share his reading notes on Soulstealers: The Chinese Sorcery Scare of 1768, and lead us in exploring a High Qing era of Kangxi and Qianlong beset with crises. Before the session, here are a few questions worth thinking about: - Do you believe souls exist? - What works of art depicting sorcery have you come across? - Novels? - Films or TV series? - Games? - What beliefs about the soul have you heard of? - What are your impressions of the Qing dynasty and the Qianlong Emperor?
