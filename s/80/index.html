The Lewis and Clark Expedition: Several Ways to Tell a Story刘易斯与克拉克远征 - 一个故事的几种讲法

烟霞第 80 期 · 2024 年Yanxia No. 80 · 2024

“刘易斯与克拉克远征”在中文资料中出现较少。但在普通美国人的历史常识中，这一事件的知名度和重要性可以类比于“唐三藏西天取经”或者“张骞出使西域”，是一件被反复讲述、称颂的事件，并被赋予了“西进运动起点”的历史意义。 本次分享，主要介绍了 - 远征的动因和过程 - 环美旅行途中可以打卡的远征相关的历史地标和博物馆 - 在多个版本的叙述中，哪些是错误信息，又有什么信息被刻意强调或隐藏了？ - 印第安女性萨卡加维亚与美国女权运动的关联是？ - 这次远征到底是否算得上成功？ - 在向印第安人“宣扬和平”的问题上，历史是如何带来了巨大的讽刺？ - 这次远征与清王朝有着了怎样的关联？ 鉴于涉及的历史背景较多，推荐参加活动前先花5分钟简单看一下：https://zh.wikipedia.org/wiki/刘易斯与克拉克远征

The Lewis and Clark Expedition rarely appears in Chinese sources. But in the historical common knowledge of ordinary Americans, its fame and importance are comparable to Xuanzang's pilgrimage to the West or Zhang Qian's mission to the Western Regions: it is an event told and celebrated again and again, and has been given the historical significance of "the starting point of westward expansion". This session mainly covers: - The motives for the expedition and how it unfolded - Historic landmarks and museums related to the expedition that you can visit on a road trip across America - Across the many versions of the story, what is misinformation, and what has been deliberately emphasised or hidden? - What is the connection between the Native American woman Sacagawea and the American women's rights movement? - Can the expedition really be counted a success? - On the question of "promoting peace" among the Native Americans, how did history deliver a huge irony? - What connection did the expedition have with the Qing dynasty? As there is a lot of historical background involved, we recommend spending five minutes skimming this before the session: https://zh.wikipedia.org/wiki/刘易斯与克拉克远征
