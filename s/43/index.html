Japanese Pure-Love Fiction日本纯爱文学

烟霞第 43 期 · 2023 年Yanxia No. 43 · 2023

本次分享将讲述以市川拓司为代表的日本纯爱文学，介绍相关的作品。 问题： 纯爱小说有什么特点？ 哪些群体的追捧早就了纯爱小说的市场？ 你如何看待纯爱小说？ 这类文学作品对现实有何参考意义？ 书籍： 《现在，很想见你》 《恋爱写真》 《在世界中心呼唤爱》 《情书》

This session covers Japanese pure-love fiction, represented by Ichikawa Takuji, and introduces related works. Questions: What are the characteristics of pure-love novels? Which readers' enthusiasm created the market for pure-love novels? What do you think of pure-love novels? What relevance do works of this kind have to real life? Books: Be With You Heavenly Forest Socrates in Love Love Letter
