很多失败的国家项目结束后，我们经常能听到这种感叹：“本意是好的，只是下面执行错了。”然而事实真的是这样吗？这一次，我们会分享詹姆斯·C·斯科特的《国家的视角》，谈一谈那些失败的国家项目。这些轰轰烈烈的运动是如何发起的？为什么他们后来都失败了？ 你看过马前卒的“睡前消息”吗？马前卒提出了很多雄心勃勃的建议：比如社会化抚养、消灭小农经济、全面普及儿童手环……你同意这些建议吗？反对的理由又是什么？ 在参加下次分享之前，我强烈推荐两个有趣的资源： 小约翰可汗的视频“英国如何给非洲人民送福利？【神奇组织08】”：https://www.bilibili.com/video/BV1uk4y1u7aY/ 豆子的微小说“改粮为桑”：https://zhuanlan.zhihu.com/p/33174367

After many failed state projects come to an end, we often hear the lament: "The intentions were good; it was just carried out badly further down." But is that really so? This time we will share James C. Scott's Seeing Like a State and talk about failed state projects. How were these grand, sweeping campaigns launched? And why did they all end in failure? Have you watched Ma Qianzu's "Bedtime News"? Ma Qianzu has put forward many ambitious proposals, such as socialised child-rearing, eliminating the smallholder economy, and universal wristbands for children. Do you agree with these proposals? And if not, what are your reasons? Before coming to the next session, I strongly recommend two interesting resources: Little John Khan's video "How Britain Delivered Welfare to the People of Africa [Amazing Organisations 08]": https://www.bilibili.com/video/BV1uk4y1u7aY/ Douzi's micro-story "Replacing Grain with Mulberry": https://zhuanlan.zhihu.com/p/33174367
