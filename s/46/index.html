《贝奥武甫》Beowulf

Beowulf《贝奥武甫》

烟霞第 46 期 · 2023 年Yanxia No. 46 · 2023

勇士们，让我们欢聚在烟霞部落的长屋里，举起牛角酒杯尽情畅饮吧！我要讲一讲高特国王贝奥武甫的故事，让诸位回到那个黑暗而奇幻的时代。1500年前，一个沼泽怪物狂暴地进入了宴会大厅，杀死了30名勇士。12年后，海平面上出现了高特人的船帆，这就是贝奥武甫传奇的开始。 啊！贝奥武甫！德意志的民族象征，丹麦人的伟大英雄。来自高特的武士国王啊，英格兰的吟游诗人仍然把你传唱！ 贝奥武甫在历史上真的存在吗？他到底是德国人，瑞典人，丹麦人，还是英国人？贝奥武甫的财宝现在在哪里？守护财宝的火龙如今在何处？这部史诗是何时被发现的？它为什么影响了如今的英国政坛？ 胜利不能靠求神拜佛，只有强壮的双臂才能赶走妖魔鬼怪。勇士们，让我们放下长剑，听一听贝奥武甫的传奇吧！ 参考书籍： 《贝奥武甫》(冯象译) 《史诗大陆：欧洲历史的血与火》(尼古拉斯·朱伯) 《牛津英国史》(肯尼斯·O·摩根) 《History of Britain and Ireland: The Definitive Visual Guide》(DK)

Warriors, let us gather in the longhouse of the Yanxia tribe, raise our drinking horns and drink our fill! I shall tell the tale of Beowulf, king of the Geats, and take you back to that dark and fantastical age. Fifteen hundred years ago, a monster from the fens burst into the mead hall in a rage and killed thirty warriors. Twelve years later, the sails of the Geats appeared on the horizon, and so the legend of Beowulf began. Ah, Beowulf! National symbol of the Germans, great hero of the Danes. Warrior king of the Geats, the bards of England still sing of you! Did Beowulf really exist? Was he German, Swedish, Danish or English? Where is Beowulf's treasure now? Where is the fire dragon that guarded it today? When was the epic discovered? And why has it influenced British politics today? Victory does not come from praying to the gods; only strong arms can drive away monsters and demons. Warriors, let us lay down our swords and hear the legend of Beowulf! Reading: Beowulf (translated by Feng Xiang) Epic Continent: Adventures in the Great Stories of Europe (Nicholas Jubber) The Oxford History of Britain (Kenneth O. Morgan) History of Britain and Ireland: The Definitive Visual Guide (DK)
