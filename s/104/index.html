阿方索九世的忧郁The Melancholy of Alfonso IX

The Melancholy of Alfonso IX阿方索九世的忧郁

烟霞第 104 期 · 2024 年Yanxia No. 104 · 2024

本次分享将视角放在八百多年前，伊比利亚地区的一位小国王身上。他被后人称之为“莱昂的阿方索九世”。 引起好奇的问题： 我们为什么要分享伊比利亚半岛的故事？ 提起西班牙，你会想到什么？ 最地道的西班牙语在什么地方？ 为什么加泰罗尼亚总是要闹独立？ 西班牙和葡萄牙是什么关系？ 将要分享的问题： 阿方索九世的个人经历，和他子孙的一些情况 阿方索九世时代莱昂王国和卡斯蒂利亚王国的现状 再征服运动（Reconquista）是如何逐步实施的 伊比利亚没能统一的历史机遇

This session turns to a minor king in Iberia more than eight hundred years ago, known to posterity as Alfonso IX of León. Questions to spark curiosity: Why should we talk about the history of the Iberian Peninsula? What comes to mind when you think of Spain? Where is the most authentic Spanish spoken? Why does Catalonia keep pushing for independence? What is the relationship between Spain and Portugal? Questions we will cover: Alfonso IX's own life, and something about his descendants The state of the kingdoms of León and Castile in Alfonso IX's time How the Reconquista was carried out step by step The historical opportunities for Iberian unification that were missed

The notes below are in Chinese, as the talks are.
