四种矛盾看文革The Cultural Revolution through Four Contradictions

The Cultural Revolution through Four Contradictions四种矛盾看文革

烟霞第 155 期 · 2026 年Yanxia No. 155 · 2026

文革常常被简单概括为“过度民主”“群众失控”甚至“多数人的暴政”。这样的解释真的足够吗？红卫兵究竟由哪些人构成？造反派在运动中是施暴者，还是后来被清算的受害者？军队在文革中究竟是秩序的维护者，还是权力斗争的参与者？文革是否只是一个特殊年代的极端事件，还是某种制度逻辑的产物？ 本次线上小型沙龙，我们将尝试用“四种矛盾”作为分析框架： - 新政权与旧体制 - 官僚与民众 - 文官与武将 - 独裁者与接班人 通过结构化梳理，理解文革的内在逻辑、权力运作与社会心理机制。本次活动不做情绪化讨论，不做简单归因。

The Cultural Revolution is often summed up simply as "too much democracy", "the masses out of control" or even "the tyranny of the majority". Are such explanations really sufficient? Who exactly made up the Red Guards? Were the rebel factions perpetrators of violence during the movement, or victims who were later purged? Was the army during the Cultural Revolution a guardian of order, or a participant in the power struggle? Was the Cultural Revolution merely an extreme event of an exceptional era, or the product of a certain institutional logic? In this small online salon, we will try using "four contradictions" as an analytical framework: - The new regime and the old system - Bureaucrats and the masses - Civil officials and military commanders - The dictator and his successors Through a structured overview, we aim to understand the internal logic of the Cultural Revolution, how power operated, and its social and psychological mechanisms. This event will avoid emotional discussion and simplistic attributions of blame.
