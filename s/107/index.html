- Picking up from last time's Cluniac reforms and the Reconquista: once "upright" men come to power, does the world become a better place? - Were the Crusaders' intentions good? What did the Crusades actually do? - Leo IX, Gregory VII, Urban II, Innocent III... do her ideals still live on? - When is the story of the film Kingdom of Heaven, and of the "iron triangle" of Catholic kings in its background, set? - Who was Saladin, another figure in that background? Why did the English and French have to pay a "Saladin tithe" on his account? - What legendary stories are told of Saint Bernard of Clairvaux? - How did the Paulicians, the Bogomils, the Albigensians (Cathars) and the Hussites develop? How was the Inquisition established in different places? - How did the Cistercians, Franciscans and Dominicans develop in this period?
