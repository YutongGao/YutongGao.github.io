Chinese Antiquities in Museums Abroad聊聊海外博物馆的中华文物

烟霞第 56 期 · 2023 年Yanxia No. 56 · 2023

本次分享以Nelson Atkins Museum 为例，分享海外博物馆观展体验以及与中国文物的复杂关系。Nelson Atkins作为海外著名的中国文物展藏地，究竟有哪些藏品？受何人支持，又如何获取如此大量精致的藏品？海外博物馆的中华藏品的意义何在？ 讨论内容如下： Nelson Atkins 如何成立？ 藏品有哪些？有什么与众不同的地方？ 观展体验如何？ 藏品从和收集而来？ 海外藏展对于博物馆的意义 什么是好的博物馆/好的观展体验？ 如何评价海外文物藏品的正当性？ 未来博物馆会逐渐萧条吗？

Taking the Nelson-Atkins Museum as an example, this session shares the experience of visiting museums abroad and their complicated relationship with Chinese antiquities. The Nelson-Atkins is famous overseas for its Chinese collection: what exactly does it hold? Who backed it, and how did it acquire so many fine pieces? What is the significance of Chinese collections in museums abroad? We will discuss: How was the Nelson-Atkins founded? What is in the collection, and what makes it distinctive? What is it like to visit? Where were the pieces collected from? What overseas collections and exhibitions mean for museums What makes a good museum, or a good museum visit? How should we judge the legitimacy of antiquities held abroad? Will museums gradually decline in the future?

The notes below are in Chinese, as the talks are.
