你好，我是高宇彤，住在加州山景城，是 Google 的一名软件工程师。

Hi, I'm Yutong Gao. I live in Mountain View, California, and work as a software engineer at Google.

工作之外，我喜欢慢慢地旅行：坐火车穿过一个国家，在寺庙、古战场和博物馆之间走走停停，读一读路边石碑上的字。2024 年冬天，我在日本待了三个多星期，从北海道一路走到九州，接着又在土耳其开车绕了一圈。

Outside work, I like to travel slowly: crossing a country by train, wandering between temples, old battlefields and museums, and stopping to read the stones by the road. In the winter of 2024 I spent three and a half weeks in Japan, from Hokkaido down to Kyushu, and then drove a loop around Turkey.

我也参与组织「烟霞」，一个主题分享活动，聊史诗、历史和文学，到现在已经办了八十多期。

I also help run Yanxia (烟霞), a series of themed talks on epics, history and literature, now more than eighty sessions in.
