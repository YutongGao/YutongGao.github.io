Adrift Like Duckweed in the Rain: The Rise and Fall of the Hawaiian Kingdom身世浮沉雨打萍——夏威夷王国的兴亡

烟霞第 84 期 · 2024 年Yanxia No. 84 · 2024

第一次去夏威夷大岛时，注意到有个浮潜点叫做Captain Cook，搜了下发现原来现在这个著名的浮潜地点就是当初库克船长发现夏威夷群岛时停靠的地方，他自己也因为部落冲突丧命于此，这片海湾还成了夏威夷文化的“禁忌之地”。后来陆续了解到这里之前曾是一个独立自主，存续近百年的独立王国，创始人还被称为“太平洋上的拿破仑”。本次分享就来聊聊夏威夷群岛上曲折的故事，同时会介绍一些相关的历史遗迹和博物馆。 - 为什么夏威夷人要碰瓷拿破仑？ - 为什么夏威夷王国成立后一直保持独立，从未成为英国殖民地，国旗却是米字旗？ - 夏威夷王国主权的丧失为什么说是一个持续了百年的过程？ - 美国如何施加对夏威夷的影响，和美国外交路线的摇摆有什么关系？ - 夏威夷的蔗糖产业如何被美国反复横跳的关税改革玩坏的？ - 夏威夷王国具体是被谁颠覆的？他们为什么有这个政治权利？具体通过什么方式？ - 华人（包括孙中山家族）如何在夏威夷发展壮大，以至于成了晚清民国革命党的重要据点？ - 现在各个岛屿分别都有什么景点/博物馆下次旅行可以顺路看看？ - 现在夏威夷人如何看待这段历史？

On my first trip to Hawaii's Big Island, I noticed a snorkelling spot called Captain Cook. Looking it up, I found that this now-famous snorkelling site was where Captain Cook anchored when he came upon the Hawaiian Islands; he himself was killed here in a clash with the islanders, and the bay became a "forbidden place" in Hawaiian culture. Later I learned that the islands had once been an independent, self-governing kingdom that lasted nearly a century, and that its founder was even called "the Napoleon of the Pacific". In this session we will talk about the twisting story of the Hawaiian Islands and introduce some related historic sites and museums. - Why did Hawaiians compare their king to Napoleon? - Why did the Hawaiian Kingdom, which remained independent from its founding and never became a British colony, have the Union Jack on its flag? - Why can the loss of Hawaiian sovereignty be described as a process that lasted a hundred years? - How did the United States exert its influence on Hawaii, and how was this tied to the swings in American foreign policy? - How was Hawaii's sugar industry wrecked by America's back-and-forth tariff reforms? - Who exactly overthrew the Hawaiian Kingdom? Why did they have the political rights to do so? By what means? - How did the Chinese (including Sun Yat-sen's family) grow and prosper in Hawaii, to the point that it became an important base for revolutionaries in the late Qing and early Republic? - What sights and museums are on each island today that you could visit on your next trip? - How do Hawaiians view this history today?

The notes below are in Chinese, as the talks are.
