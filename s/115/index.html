The 2024 Nobel Prize in Economics: The Origins of Income Gaps between Nations2024诺贝尔经济学奖——国家间收入差距的起源

烟霞第 115 期 · 2024 年Yanxia No. 115 · 2024

今年诺贝尔经济学奖授予了达龙·阿西莫格鲁（Daron Acemoglu）、西蒙·约翰逊（Simon Johnson）和詹姆斯·罗宾逊（James Robinson），奖励他们关于制度如何形成并影响经济繁荣研究领域的突出贡献。三位诺奖得主的研究成果区别于之前曾经分享过的彭慕兰在《大分流》一书中的观点，也在过去二十年中引发了各国历史、政治、经济学者的激烈辩论。本周的分享将简单介绍他们的研究成果，并讨论我们应如何理解及看待经济学的研究成果。 引起兴趣的问题： - 诺贝尔奖为什么会包括经济学？ - 诺贝尔经济学奖在实际生活中有什么应用？ - 为什么有些曾经的欧洲殖民地成为了现代经济强国，而有些殖民地则至今社会动荡？ 将会探讨的问题： - 殖民制度如何影响现代国家经济发展？ - 世界上发达的古文明地区在近现代的“财富逆转”为何发生？ - 如何看待产权制度在经济发展中起到的作用？

This year's Nobel Prize in Economics was awarded to Daron Acemoglu, Simon Johnson and James Robinson for their outstanding contributions to research on how institutions are formed and how they affect prosperity. The laureates' findings differ from the view Kenneth Pomeranz put forward in The Great Divergence, which we discussed in an earlier session, and over the past twenty years they have sparked fierce debate among historians, political scientists and economists around the world. This week's session will briefly introduce their research and discuss how we should understand and regard the findings of economics. Questions to spark interest: - Why is there a Nobel Prize in economics at all? - What practical uses does Nobel-winning economics have in real life? - Why did some former European colonies become modern economic powers, while others remain in turmoil to this day? Questions we will explore: - How did colonial institutions shape the economic development of modern nations? - Why did the world's advanced ancient civilisations undergo a "reversal of fortune" in the modern era? - What role do property rights play in economic development?

The notes below are in Chinese, as the talks are.
