Some warm-up questions: What places made the deepest impression on you when you travelled in Seoul, or what would you like to see there? What do you know about the Second Republic and Park Chung-hee? The May 16 coup was a military coup launched on 16 May 1961 by Major General Park Chung-hee, then deputy commander of the South Korean Army's Second Army. It came little more than a year after the April 19 Revolution had brought down Syngman Rhee's government. Political power was then in the hands of the newly formed Democratic Party government under Prime Minister Chang Myon, but its grip on society was weak, and the economy and people's livelihoods had not noticeably improved. The coup ended the short-lived Second Republic and the Democratic Party government, and brought Park Chung-hee to power. It interrupted South Korea's constitutional order and began Park's eighteen years of rule and thirty-two years of military government in South Korea, while also laying the groundwork for the country's economic take-off. This session will tell the story of the May 16 coup, introduce the forces involved at the time, explore why the coup succeeded, and share some Seoul sightseeing spots connected with it. Questions we plan to discuss: The political forces in South Korea at the time: the politicians (the new and old factions of the Democratic Party), the military, the rebel troops, and the US forces How the coup unfolded Why the coup succeeded Sightseeing spots in Seoul connected with the May 16 coup: The Blue House (formerly the residence of the Japanese Governor-General of Korea) The buildings along Sejong-ro: the former Capitol (the Japanese Government-General Building, now demolished; the site is now part of Gyeongbokgung Palace), the former Supreme Council for National Reconstruction building (now the National Museum of Korean Contemporary History), the former National Assembly building (the Keijō Public Hall under Japanese rule, now the Seoul Metropolitan Council), Deoksugung Palace, the former Seoul City Hall, and the former Bando Hotel (now demolished) Sungnyemun Hangang Bridge Seoul National Cemetery

你对第二共和国和朴正熙有什么了解？

“5.16军事政变”是1961年5月16日时任韩国陆军第二野战军副司令官朴正熙少将发动的一场军事政变。这次政变发生在四一九革命推翻李承晚政权后仅一年多之后，当时新成立的以张勉为内阁总理的民主党政权掌握了政治权力，但对社会的控制能力有限，且韩国的经济民生状况并未显著改善。

政变终结了短暂的“第二共和国”时期和民主党政府，同时促使朴正熙上台。这次政变中断了韩国的宪政秩序，开启了朴正熙长达18年的统治以及韩国32年的军人执政，同时也为韩国经济的腾飞创造了前提条件。
