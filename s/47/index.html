沧浪亭疑案The Mystery of the Canglang Pavilion

The Mystery of the Canglang Pavilion沧浪亭疑案

烟霞第 47 期 · 2023 年Yanxia No. 47 · 2023

苏州最古老的园林沧浪亭是如何得名的？ 始建于北宋中期的沧浪亭后来有着怎样的命运？它与庆历新政、变法兴衰、宋室南渡有着怎样的关联？ 这座旷世园林见证了何种家族兴衰？它的几任主人又经历了怎样的身世浮沉？ 时过境迁，曾经的故事已经湮没于岁月的长河，传世的文献中也遗失了详细的记载，历史却仍在空白处留下了蛛丝马迹……

How did the Canglang Pavilion, Suzhou's oldest garden, get its name? Built in the middle of the Northern Song, what became of the Canglang Pavilion later? How was it connected with the Qingli Reforms, the rise and fall of the reform movement, and the Song court's flight south? What rises and falls of families did this matchless garden witness? And what reversals of fortune did its successive owners go through? Times have changed, and the old stories have sunk into the long river of years; the surviving texts have lost the detailed records, yet history has still left faint traces in the gaps...
