上野战争，以及芥川龙之介The Battle of Ueno, and Akutagawa Ryūnosuke

The Battle of Ueno, and Akutagawa Ryūnosuke上野战争，以及芥川龙之介

烟霞第 114 期 · 2024 年Yanxia No. 114 · 2024

本次分享将简单介绍戊辰战争中上野战争的一段故事，以及共读芥川龙之介的一篇相关小说《阿富的贞操》。我们之前曾经读过芥川龙之介的《鼻子》，也讲述过会津战争的一段故事。这次可以说是让前后故事更加完整，也算是了结了一个心愿。 - 江户开城之后，为什么会爆发上野战争？ - 彰义队起到了什么作用？ - 小说《阿富的贞操》在讲什么？ - 为什么日本演员西田敏行出演西乡隆盛之后会遭受家乡人的唾骂？ - 《阿富的贞操》中部分情节和观点可能不完全符合现代女性主义的价值观，希望大家可以批判地进行讨论和审视。

This session will briefly tell the story of the Battle of Ueno in the Boshin War, and we will read together a related short story by Akutagawa Ryūnosuke, "The Chastity of Otomi". We have previously read Akutagawa's "The Nose" and told a story from the Aizu War, so this session rounds out those earlier stories and fulfils a long-held wish. - Why did the Battle of Ueno break out after the surrender of Edo Castle? - What role did the Shōgitai play? - What is the story "The Chastity of Otomi" about? - Why was the Japanese actor Nishida Toshiyuki vilified by people from his home region after playing Saigō Takamori? - Some of the plot and views in "The Chastity of Otomi" may not fully accord with modern feminist values; we hope everyone will discuss and examine them critically.
