日本的世界观：200年来的变化Japan's View of the World: Two Hundred Years of Change

Japan's View of the World: Two Hundred Years of Change日本的世界观：200年来的变化

烟霞第 50 期 · 2023 年Yanxia No. 50 · 2023

本次分享来自剑桥日本史主编马里乌斯詹森一本小书《日本的世界观：200年来的变化》。主要选取了德川幕府时期，明治维新时期和二战前后的三个时间切片，探讨日本人在这三个时代对于世界的认识，产生的变化以及对日本后续发展的意义。虽然听起来话题很大，但切入点很具体。詹森每个时代都选取了一个不那么知名的人物，从他们的个人经历入手，介绍了那个时代日本人是如何认知世界的。本次分享这次分享相对侧重17和18世纪的内容。 一些值得探讨的问题： 近代以前日本人如何看待中国的政权和欧洲的政权？ 日本知识分子如何看待中国文化？他们如何认知自己在中国文化圈中的位置？ 德川幕府时期为什么会有一小撮日本人通晓荷兰语并且翻译了大量西方科学著作？这些人的事业对后来日本经历的剧变有什么影响么？ 鸦片战争之后，黑船来航之前日本是否意识到了世界的变化？他们有什么对策？ 为什么明治维新之后核心领导层选择远离政治中心去周游列国一年多时间？他们都做了什么？见到了谁？这对后来明治维新的路线选择有影响吗？ 日本明治维新之后崛起的日本如何重新认识自己在东亚的地位？ 日本战后对世界的认知经历了什么样的变化？日本人如何看待美军基地/安保协定/宪法第九条对日本现在的影响？

This session draws on a short book by Marius Jansen, general editor of The Cambridge History of Japan: Japan and Its World: Two Centuries of Change. It takes three slices of time, the Tokugawa shogunate, the Meiji Restoration, and the years around the Second World War, and explores how the Japanese of each era understood the world, how that understanding changed, and what it meant for Japan's later development. Although the topic sounds vast, the approach is very concrete: for each era Jansen picks a less famous figure and, starting from that person's own experiences, shows how the Japanese of the time perceived the world. This session will focus relatively more on the seventeenth and eighteenth centuries. Some questions worth exploring: Before the modern era, how did the Japanese view the Chinese and European powers? How did Japanese intellectuals view Chinese culture? How did they see their own place within the Chinese cultural sphere? Why, under the Tokugawa shogunate, did a small group of Japanese know Dutch and translate a large number of Western scientific works? Did their work have any influence on the upheavals Japan later went through? After the Opium War but before the arrival of the Black Ships, was Japan aware that the world was changing? What did they do about it? Why, after the Meiji Restoration, did the core leadership choose to leave the political centre and tour the world for more than a year? What did they do, and whom did they meet? Did this affect the course the Meiji Restoration later took? How did Japan, rising after the Meiji Restoration, rethink its position in East Asia? How did Japan's understanding of the world change after the war? How do the Japanese see the effects of the US bases, the security treaty and Article 9 of the constitution on Japan today?
